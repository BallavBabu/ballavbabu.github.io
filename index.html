Lila Bula Busal, PhD

My research is in development economics, with a focus on structural change. I study why some industries grow faster than others and how these changes affect people’s livelihoods. Much of my empirical work focuses on South Asia and the wider Asia-Pacific region.

Researcher, Yokohama National University

Data & Software

Data

Harmonized ADB multi-regional input-output panel

Current-price tables for 35 sectors in 62 economies plus the rest of the world. The panel covers 19 years: 2000 and 2007–2024.

ADB data for the adbmrio package

Preprocessed matrices for bilateral trade and value-added analysis in R, covering benchmark years between 2000 and 2023.

R packages

gvcAnalyzer

I created and maintain this R package for measuring value-added trade and participation in global value chains.

Software citation

adbmrio

I developed this R package to decompose bilateral exports and account for embodied emissions using ADB tables.
