Lila Ballav Bhusal

PhD in Economics, Yokohama National University

I am an applied economist working on input-output analysis, global value chains and the carbon embodied in trade, with a focus on South Asia and the Asia-Pacific. My recent work assigns CO₂ emissions in the global textile and clothing chain to the incomes they generate, measures how far upstream emissions sit in Asia-Pacific production, and traces remittances through social accounting matrices for Nepal, India and Bangladesh.

I also build open tools for this work, including the gvcAnalyzer R package on CRAN and a harmonized panel of ADB multiregional input-output tables.

I am seeking academic and research positions in Japan and internationally.
