About

I am a quantitative economist studying how trade and production networks shape economic development and environmental outcomes. My research combines multi-regional input–output models, structural decomposition and applied econometrics, with a focus on South Asia and the wider Asia–Pacific region.

I am a Research Fellow at Yokohama National University and a Research Assistant at JICA’s Ogata Sadako Research Institute for Peace and Development. I completed my Ph.D. in Economics at Yokohama National University in 2025.

Alongside my journal publications, I develop R software and prepare open datasets for reproducible economic analysis. My interests span global value chains, value-added trade, embodied emissions and sectoral change.

Projects & Open Data

Software Development (R Packages)

gvcAnalyzer

CRAN Accepted

Role: Author & Maintainer

An R package on CRAN for decomposing global value chain participation and value-added trade. It implements the frameworks of Borin and Mancini, and of Borin, Mancini and Taglioni.

DOI: 10.32614/CRAN.package.gvcAnalyzer

adbmrio

GitHub / Dev

Role: Developer

An R package in development for working with Asian Development Bank (ADB) MRIO tables, including bilateral export decomposition and embodied CO2 accounting.

Install via devtools: devtools::install_github("BallavBabu/adbmrio")

Harmonized Datasets

Harmonized ADB MRIO Panel: Current Prices (2000–2024)

Zenodo Data Repository (2025)

A panel of ADB multi-regional input–output tables in current prices, assembled across releases to support analysis of production networks over time.

Access Data (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17694852)

ADB MRIO Panel Data (2000–2023) for adbmrio Package

Zenodo Data Repository (2025)

The pre-processed input data required for the adbmrio R package. This dataset includes the necessary matrices for 63 economies and 35 sectors, optimized for computational efficiency within the R environment.

Access Data (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17671302)
