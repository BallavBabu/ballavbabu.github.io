Research

Production networks.
Economic and environmental outcomes.

I examine where value is created in international production, how sectoral change contributes to development, and how economic gains relate to environmental pressures. I also work with time-series and panel data to study questions in applied economics.

01 / TRADE & DEVELOPMENT

Global value chains

Measuring participation in production networks, value-added trade and the sectoral structure of developing economies.

02 / ECONOMY & ENVIRONMENT

Carbon and economic activity

Tracing embodied emissions through supply chains and examining their relationship with income and value added.

03 / METHODS & APPLICATIONS

Quantitative economic analysis

Using input–output models, structural decomposition and econometric methods to investigate sectoral and macroeconomic change.

Research practiceMulti-regional input–output databases · Reproducible analysis · R package development · Data visualisation
